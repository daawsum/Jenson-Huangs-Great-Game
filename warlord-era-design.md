# Warlord Era — design

## Intent
A standalone Victoria 2 (3.04, Heart of Darkness) multiplayer mod. China is shattered into 35 civilized warlord states, 20 of them built as balanced player slots. Nobody else on Earth is civilized. The warlords race each other to conquer and colonize the world.

## Goals
- 35 warlord tags, all civilized; 20 "major" ones sized and balanced for players, 15 "minor" ones as smaller buffers and early targets.
- China subdivided: roughly twice as many Chinese provinces, with new province IDs, regrouped into new states.
- Rest of the world is a mix of empty colonizable land and fragmented weak states, none civilized.
- Colonial race is live from day one.
- Loads cleanly and stays in sync in multiplayer (everyone uses the same zipped mod folder).

## Non-goals
- Hand-drawn province borders. New borders are generated by script.
- New events, decisions or flavor chains. Vanilla events tied to removed countries are disabled, not rewritten.
- AI balance. This is tuned for humans on the 20 majors.

## Approaches considered
1. **Vanilla base, scripted map split (chosen).** Smallest checksum surface, no dependency on another mod's updates, every player already has the base files.
2. **Build on HPM/GFM.** More Chinese provinces already exist, but their event and decision webs assume the historical world and would break or need gutting.
3. **No new provinces, only regroup states.** Much less work, but you asked for new provinces and IDs.

## Design

### Map
- Every province of China proper is split into 2 (the largest into 3) by repainting `provinces.bmp`. About 150 new provinces get IDs from 3249 up; `max_provinces`, `definition.csv`, `positions.txt`, `region.txt`, `continent.txt`, `climate.txt`, terrain and adjacency follow.
- Population, RGOs and life rating are inherited from the parent province; pops are divided by area.
- New provinces are named after real prefectures and counties where I can place them, otherwise "North/South <parent>".
- States are regrouped to 3–4 provinces each, giving about 75 Chinese states instead of 32.

### Warlords (all civilized)
Major, player slots (20): Zhili, Fengtian, Anhui Clique, Shanxi, Guominjun (Shaanxi), Shandong, Henan, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Fujian, Guangdong, Guangxi, Hunan, Hubei, Jiangxi, Sichuan (Chengdu), Yunnan, Guizhou, Ma Clique (Gansu), Xinjiang.

Minor (15): Jilin, Heilongjiang, Rehe, Chahar, Suiyuan, Ningxia, Qinghai, Xikang, Chongqing (East Sichuan), Tibet, Mongolia, Hainan, Taiwan, Shanghai, Tannu Tuva.

- Majors start with comparable population and industry score, a port or a land route to one, and the same tech package.
- Victoria 2 cannot lock countries in the lobby, so "playable" means built for a player; minors are still selectable.
- All warlords hold cores on all of China, so unification wars are possible.
- All get colonial techs and a starting fleet, so colonizing starts immediately.

### Rest of the world
- **Start date** stays 1836 (the engine only runs 1836–1936); the setting is alternate-history.
- **Empty and colonizable:** sub-Saharan Africa, the Americas outside a few native states, Oceania, Siberia, Central Asia steppe. Life ratings raised so nothing is locked behind late tech.
- **Partly westernized (uncivilized, some reforms, decent armies):** fragmented Europe (German and Italian minors, the larger powers broken into their releasable nations), Japan, Ottoman successor states.
- **Uncivilized (no reforms):** Indian princely states, Persia, Arabia, Southeast Asia, Korea, Ethiopia, North African states.
- **Primitive (no reforms, minimal tech, tiny armies):** a scattering of native states in the Americas, Africa and the Pacific.

### Packaging
- Folder `mod/Warlord Era` plus `Warlord Era.mod`, with history folders fully replaced. Installed into your game's `mod` folder and also zipped for the other players.

## Left to the builder
Exact province cut lines, province names, starting army sizes, flag art (simple generated flags), country colors.

## Changes agreed after the first draft (as built)
- Rest of the world is a mix: empty land plus fragmented weak states.
- Mongolia is no longer a civilized minor clique. It is partly westernized, heavily buffed and playable. Kashgar and Xuzhou cliques fill out the 15 minors; Tannu Tuva was dropped.
- Sikh Empire: partly westernized, heavily buffed, playable. Persia: partly westernized, moderately buffed, playable.
- Migration follows the Vae Victis flag system: South East Asian states receive emigrants until the first overseas dominion is chartered, then the dominions do.
- Colonial puppets: 17 "Charter the Dominion of ..." decisions, each with a follow-up decision to hand over later conquests.
- 97 decisions in total, including 16 new formables.
- Warlords do not hold cores on each other. Instead two always-available war goals exist between cliques (take a state, absorb a small clique).
- Major cliques' populations are scaled into a 12-24 million band for balance.
