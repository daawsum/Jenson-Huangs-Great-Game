# Jenson Huang's Great Game

A multiplayer mod for Victoria 2 (3.04, Heart of Darkness). China has westernised and broken into
35 warlord cliques, 20 of them built to be played; the rest of the world is partially westernised,
uncivilized or empty and open to colonisation.

## What is in it

- 35 Chinese cliques on 137 new provinces and 92 new states, each major with its own culture,
  rival, partner, modifiers and decisions.
- The Sikh Empire, Persia and Mongolia as playable uncivilized powers.
- Western and central Europe as one backward Holy Roman Empire.
